Paintroid removes READ_MEDIA_IMAGES from its manifests and runtime gate.
System-picker imports/replacements and modern saves need no broad photo access.
Legacy READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE and WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE are capped at API 28.

Paintroid validation: 265 unit tests, 3 FileIO tests on Android 14, and
PMD, Checkstyle, lint and Detekt passed. Catroid integration/device checks
are tracked separately in the Catroid pull request.
